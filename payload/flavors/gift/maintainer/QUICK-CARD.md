# For you, Paco.

**In this box is a workshop.**

The mini PC runs an AI engineer that writes code, designs systems, and ships products. When you say *"I want to build X"*, it builds it with you — from your phone, your laptop, anywhere.

Two projects are already written, waiting for you to start.

---

**To begin:** plug in power, Ethernet, monitor and a USB keyboard. Press power. Follow what the screen tells you. About 25 minutes from plug to building.

Longer guide is on the USB stick and inside the device at `~/README.md`.
