# Eval — no-ai-slop

Use after editing. Each check is pass/fail. If any fails, fix the draft before returning it. For detection requests: check that the answer names each pattern with a quoted line and a short fix, without rewriting.

## Principles

1. Does the edit keep the author's point without adding claims, examples, numbers, or opinions?
2. Does it preserve their distinctive vocabulary, cadence, humor, hedges, digressions, and level of polish?
3. Are the strong human sentences still intact, without homogenizing every paragraph?
4. Is the trimming proportional to the actual slop, with no aggressive compression that erases character?
5. Does the text get to the point where it should, keeping the personal preamble that adds context or character?
6. Does every sentence earn its place: concrete facts, protected data, direct verbs?
7. Active voice with human subjects where possible?
8. Are the useful edge and the structure preserved, unless they hurt the piece?

## Words and patterns

1. Are AI filler words, empty adverbs, and bloated phrases gone (except verbatim quotes)?
2. Are binary contrasts, negative listings, rhetorical setups, and throat-clearing openers gone?
3. Are fake exclusivity, colon reveals, trailing analytical participles, cardboard verbs, synonym cycling, dramatic fragmentation, and robotic rhythm fixed?
4. Are importance puffery and weasel attribution replaced with facts and named sources — or flagged for the author when there's no source?
5. Are fake-deep closers deleted (not rewritten as better metaphors) and summary endings cut, ending on something concrete?
6. Is formatting slop gone: emojis in headings, decorative bold, bullets that should be prose, headers over tiny sections?
7. Are em dashes (—) used sparingly: none in short copy, 1-2 max in long texts?

## Final read

1. Does the draft avoid robotic symmetry, repeated sentence shapes, and bursts of punch-sentences?
2. Would the author recognize the edited text as their own voice?
3. Would it sound natural read aloud to a sharp colleague?
