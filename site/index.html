Your own AI dev box.

Secure by default: zero public ports, everything behind Tailscale. One command, ~15 minutes to coding from your phone — on any Ubuntu 24.04 machine you own.

curl -fsSL https://buildersinabox.com/install.sh | sudo bash
View on GitHub Read the script

What you get:

  • Claude Code (or another AI CLI) reachable from your phone over Tailscale — no port forwarding, no public IP, nothing listening on the open internet.
  • A tmux session that survives disconnects. Pick up from the couch, a coffee shop, a flight.
  • A scaffolded workspace and a guided tutorial that gets you building your first project.

Bring your own Ubuntu 24.04 box — a mini PC, a homelab VM, or a VPS. You'll also need a paid Claude subscription (Pro or above) or a Google account for Antigravity CLI; Tailscale's free plan is plenty. Open source, MIT.

The README lists everything the installer changes and the security model; --uninstall removes it all.

Just want Claude working in the background? Anthropic's Cowork does that without a server. This is for when the agent needs your machine — your files, your LAN, your credentials, your cron jobs.
